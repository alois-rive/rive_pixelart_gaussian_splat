A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a small wooden mine cart on four iron wheels, heaped with gems.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- Each side view is exactly frontal to its side: no other face of the object shows in it (a slightly oblique view comes out wider and fails the aspect gate).
- The four panels show the SAME object, only turned: FRONT: the cart's front end faces the camera (we look along the rails it rolls on, so we see its narrow end). RIGHT: the camera has walked round to the cart's own right-hand side, so we see the cart's long side and its front points to the LEFT edge of the image. BACK: seen from behind (the other narrow end). TOP: seen from straight above, looking down, with its front towards the BOTTOM edge of the image and no wall showing. Each detail appears only in the views from which it is really visible.
- One flat, uniform, mid grey background (#9a9a9a) everywhere: no floor, no rails, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views):
- A tub shaped like an upside-down truncated pyramid: open top 1.05 m long (front to back) and 0.75 m wide, bottom 0.85 m long and 0.6 m wide, 0.45 m tall, its bottom 0.25 m above the ground. Wooden plank walls.
- Iron bands: a flat iron strip along the whole top rim, and one vertical iron strip down each of the four corners.
- Four chunky iron wheels, octagonal, 0.32 m across and 0.1 m thick, two on each side under the tub, their centres 0.3 m in front of and behind the tub's middle, standing on the ground line. Short iron axles between each pair.
- A heap of big faceted gems and ore chunks filling the tub, rising 0.15 m above the rim in a low mound.
- A small iron lantern hanging from a short hook at the middle of the FRONT rim, its glass glowing.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 300 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no tiny details, no plank lines drawn, no bolts, no texture noise, no grain.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours (a 4-colour palette, EN4): wood walls #e5b083 (lit) and #426e5d (shade), iron bands, wheels, axles and lantern frame #20283d, gems #fbf7f3 with a few #e5b083 facets, lantern glass #fbf7f3.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
