A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a tiny chunky open-top racing roadster with its driver sitting in it, for a seaside village racing game.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- The car is long: the RIGHT side view is NOT a copy of the front view. In the RIGHT view the car is about 1.8 times as long as it is tall overall and shows all its length, the nose pointing to the LEFT edge of the image; the FRONT and BACK views are narrow (the car's width only).
- Every view sits whole inside its panel with a clear margin of background all round (at least a tenth of the panel): nothing touches or crosses a panel edge.
- Each side view is exactly frontal to its side: no other face of the object shows in it (a slightly oblique view comes out wider and fails the aspect gate).
- The four panels show the SAME object, only turned: FRONT: the car's nose faces the camera, we look straight at the grille and the driver's face. RIGHT: the camera has walked round to the side that was on the RIGHT of the image in the front view (the car's own left-hand side), so the car's nose points to the LEFT edge of the image. BACK: seen from behind (the tail and the back of the driver's helmet). TOP: seen from straight above, looking down, with the nose towards the BOTTOM edge of the image and no side wall showing. Each detail appears only in the views from which it is really visible.
- One flat, uniform, light grey background (#9a9a9a) everywhere: no floor, no road, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no numbers, no letters, no logos, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views), 2.4 m long, 1.6 m wide across the wheels, 1.25 m tall to the top of the helmet:
- Body: a smooth-pebble-like rounded hull made of big flat facets, 2.3 m long, 1.2 m wide, its top 0.7 m above the ground, its underside 0.2 m above the ground. Octagonal cross-section: chamfered top edges. The nose is short and rounded, a little lower than the tail.
- Nose: a dark oval grille in the middle of the front face and two round headlamps either side of it.
- Racing stripe: one broad cream stripe 0.35 m wide running down the middle of the body from the nose, over the top, to the tail (around the cockpit).
- Cockpit: one open seat in the middle of the body, slightly behind the centre, with a small curved windscreen in front of it.
- Headrest fairing: a long rounded hump behind the driver's head, in the body colour, tapering to the tail.
- Wheels: four chunky dark tyres 0.6 m across and 0.3 m wide, at the four corners, half outside the body, with light grey hubs. The front wheels are 0.4 m from the nose, the rear ones 0.4 m from the tail.
- Driver: seen from the chest up in the cockpit: dark blue jacket shoulders, a big round white helmet (0.4 m across) with a red stripe over its top from front to back, and dark blue goggles across the front of the helmet.
- One short round exhaust pipe stub sticking straight out of the middle of the tail, low down.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render, like a chunky toy car in a PlayStation-era racing game: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 600 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no tiny details, no panel lines, no bolts, no mirrors, no texture noise, no grain.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours (the Zughy 32 palette): body #e6482e (red-orange) with #a93b3b on the shaded underside; stripe #dff6f5; tyres #39314b; hubs #a0938e; grille #302c2e; headlamps #f4cca1; windscreen #8aebf1; jacket and goggles #394778; helmet #dff6f5 with its stripe #e6482e; exhaust #7d7071.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
