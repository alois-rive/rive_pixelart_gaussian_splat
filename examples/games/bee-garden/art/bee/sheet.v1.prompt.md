A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a chubby cartoon bumblebee, the player character of a garden game.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and floating at the same height in the three side views.
- Each side view is exactly frontal to its side: no other face of the object shows in it (a slightly oblique view comes out wider and fails the aspect gate).
- The four panels show the SAME object, only turned: FRONT: the bee faces the camera (we see its face, eyes and antennae). RIGHT: the camera has walked round to the bee's own right-hand side (not the image's right), so the bee's head points to the LEFT edge of the image. BACK: seen from behind (the stinger towards the camera). TOP: seen from straight above, looking down, with its head towards the BOTTOM edge of the image. Each detail appears only in the views from which it is really visible.
- One flat, uniform, mid grey background (#9a9a9a) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views), total about 0.95 m long (head to stinger), 0.6 m wide, 0.75 m tall including wings:
- Abdomen: a fat faceted egg shape 0.65 m long, 0.55 m wide, 0.5 m tall, behind the head, with three wide dark bands wrapped around it (bands as wide as the yellow gaps between them).
- Head: a faceted ball 0.4 m wide at the front of the abdomen, slightly lower than the abdomen's top.
- Two big round dark eyes on the head's front, wide apart. A small smile is NOT needed.
- Two thick antennae rising from the top of the head, curving forward, each ending in a ball 0.08 m wide.
- Two wings: big flat rounded teardrop plates 0.5 m long and 0.3 m wide, rising from the top of the abdomen just behind the head, spread in a V up and out to the left and right (about 35 degrees from vertical), slightly swept back.
- A short dark cone stinger at the very back.
- No legs.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 400 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no fur, no tiny details, no texture noise, no grain.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours (an 8-colour palette, FunkyFuture 8): body yellow #ffda45 (lit) and #ff8142 (shade); bands, eyes and stinger #2b0f54; antennae #2b0f54 with #ff4f69 ball tips; wings #49e7ec with a #fff7f8 inner panel.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
