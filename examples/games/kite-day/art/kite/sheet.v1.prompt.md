A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a classic diamond kite, as a chunky toy-like 3D object (no tail, no string).

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- Each side view is exactly frontal to its side: no other face of the object shows in it (a slightly oblique view comes out wider and fails the aspect gate).
- The four panels show the SAME object, only turned: FRONT: the object faces the camera. RIGHT: the camera has walked round to the object's own right-hand side (not the image's right), so the object faces the LEFT edge of the image. BACK: seen from behind. TOP: seen from straight above, looking down, with its front towards the BOTTOM edge of the image and no wall showing. Each detail appears only in the views from which it is really visible (a part seen side-on in one view is seen end-on in another).
- One flat, uniform background (#d9d9d9) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views), standing upright like a kite in the air, its face towards the FRONT view:
- A diamond (rhombus) sail 1.0 m wide and 1.3 m tall, its widest point 0.4 m below the top tip. The sail is a thick panel (0.08 m) bowed backwards: its left and right tips bend 0.18 m back, so in the TOP view it is a shallow V whose centre is nearest the BOTTOM edge of the image and whose tips bend towards the TOP.
- The sail is split into four triangular panels by the spars, in two colours: the top-left and bottom-right panels pink #ee8695, the top-right and bottom-left panels pale peach #fbbbad.
- Two chunky dark wooden spars (0.07 m square), a vertical one from tip to tip and a horizontal one across the widest point, standing proud on the FRONT face of the sail.
- A small square knot block (0.12 m) where the spars cross, and a short stub (0.15 m) at the bottom tip where a tail would tie.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 100 to 300 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no tiny details, no texture noise, no grain, no fabric folds.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours: sail panels pink #ee8695 and pale peach #fbbbad, spars, knot and stub dark #292831.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
