# Penguin, phase 1: blockout of every named part at the spec's sizes (penguin.spec.md). Up Z,
# facing -Y (the head), origin on the ground under the body's centre. 0.80 m beak tip to feet.

# body: a low 8-sided egg lying on its belly, 0.36 wide, 0.66 long, 0.26 tall

apply --location
