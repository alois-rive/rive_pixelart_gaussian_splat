A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a cute penguin belly-sliding, lying flat on its belly with its head raised and looking forward, flippers swept back along its sides.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and lying on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- A WIDE EMPTY MARGIN in every panel: the object fills at most 65 percent of its panel's width and height; the beak, the feet and the flippers never come near the panel edges (in the RIGHT view the whole penguin, beak tip to feet, is smaller than in the other panels' widest dimension would suggest: keep it well inside).
- Each side view is exactly frontal to its side: no other face of the object shows in it.
- The four panels show the SAME penguin, only turned: FRONT: the penguin's face looks straight at the camera (we see its head and beak head-on, the long body hidden behind the head). RIGHT: the camera has walked round to the penguin's own right-hand side, so the penguin's head points to the LEFT edge of the image and its tail and feet to the right edge. BACK: seen from behind (tail and feet towards the camera, the head behind). TOP: seen from straight above, looking down, with its head towards the BOTTOM edge of the image.
- One flat, uniform, mid grey background (#9a9a9a) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views):
- Body: a long rounded egg-shaped torso lying horizontally on its belly, 0.70 m long (head to tail), 0.32 m wide, 0.24 m tall. Dark back, a pale belly underneath that shows as a pale band along the lower edge in the side view.
- Head: at the front end, raised: the top of the head 0.34 m above the ground, a pale face patch around the eyes, a short pointed wedge beak 0.08 m long pointing forward.
- Flippers: two flat flippers 0.25 m long, swept backwards along the sides of the body and slightly out, one on each side; in the RIGHT view the near flipper shows as a dark flat wing lying along the side of the body.
- Feet: two small flat feet sticking out behind the tail, 0.1 m each.
- A short knitted scarf around the neck, with one end trailing back over the penguin's LEFT shoulder (not the right).

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 400 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no feathers drawn, no tiny details, no texture noise, no grain.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours (a 4-colour palette, Mist GB): back, head and flippers #2d1b00; belly and face patch #c4f0c2; beak and feet #5ab9a8; scarf #1e606e.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
