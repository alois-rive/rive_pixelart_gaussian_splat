A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a chunky pirate ship's cannon on a wooden carriage with four small wheels.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- Each side view is exactly frontal to its side: no other face of the object shows in it (a slightly oblique view comes out wider and fails the aspect gate).
- The four panels show the SAME object, only turned: FRONT: the object faces the camera. RIGHT: the camera has walked round to the object's own right-hand side (not the image's right), so the object faces the LEFT edge of the image. BACK: seen from behind. TOP: seen from straight above, looking down, with its front towards the BOTTOM edge of the image and no wall showing. Each detail appears only in the views from which it is really visible (a part seen side-on in one view is seen end-on in another).
- One flat, uniform background (#d9d9d9) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views):
- A heavy iron barrel, 1.5 m long and 0.45 m across at the breech, narrowing to 0.36 m at the muzzle, with a thicker ring at the muzzle and a round knob at the back end. The barrel points straight at the camera in the FRONT view (we look into the muzzle, a dark round hole) and is raised slightly (10 degrees). Its trunnions rest on the carriage cheeks at the barrel's middle.
- A low wooden carriage, 1.1 m long (front to back), 0.8 m wide, 0.5 m tall: two thick side cheeks (0.12 m thick boards with a stepped top edge, higher at the front) joined by a front and a rear axle block.
- Four small solid wooden wheels, 0.36 m across and 0.1 m thick, two on each side, on the front and the rear axles.
- A small iron fuse ring on top of the barrel's back end.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 300 to 600 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no tiny details, no texture noise, no grain, no plank lines.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours: barrel iron #203c56 with the muzzle ring and knob #544e68, carriage wood #8d697a, wheels #d08159, axle blocks #544e68, the muzzle hole #0d2b45.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
