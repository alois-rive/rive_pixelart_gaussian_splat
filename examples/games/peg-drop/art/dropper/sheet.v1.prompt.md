A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a small clockwork coin dropper, a brass machine that drops gold coins out of a chute at its bottom.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- Every view sits whole inside its panel with a clear margin of background all round (at least a tenth of the panel): nothing touches or crosses a panel edge.
- Each side view is exactly frontal to its side: no other face of the object shows in it (a slightly oblique view comes out wider and fails the aspect gate).
- The four panels show the SAME object, only turned: FRONT: the object faces the camera. RIGHT: the camera has walked round to the side that was on the RIGHT of the image in the front view (the object's own left-hand side), so the object faces the LEFT edge of the image; the wind-up key, which is on the object's own RIGHT side, is on the far side and only its tip may peek out. BACK: seen from behind. TOP: seen from straight above, looking down, with its front towards the BOTTOM edge of the image and no wall showing. Each detail appears only in the views from which it is really visible (a part seen side-on in one view is seen end-on in another).
- One flat, uniform, mid grey background (#a9a9a9) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views):
- The body: a chunky brass box 1.0 m wide, 0.7 m deep and 0.55 m tall with bevelled vertical corners, its bottom 0.3 m above the ground.
- The hopper: an eight-sided copper funnel on top of the body, 0.8 m across at its open rim and 0.45 m across where it meets the body, 0.4 m tall; one big gold coin (0.45 m across, 0.08 m thick) stands upright in it, its upper half showing above the rim, its face towards the front.
- The chute: a short eight-sided brass tube under the middle of the body, 0.32 m across, reaching from the body down to the ground line.
- On the FRONT face: one big flat gear wheel 0.42 m across with eight chunky teeth, darker brass, standing 0.05 m proud of the face, centred.
- A wind-up key on the object's own RIGHT side (the image's LEFT in the FRONT view): a steel stem 0.2 m long sticking straight out of the side, ending in a flat key bow 0.45 m tall made of two round lobes, the bow's flat side facing sideways.
- Total height 1.25 m, total width with the key 1.35 m.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 500 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no tiny details (no screws, no rivets, no engraving), no texture noise, no grain, no writing.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours (from the Pear36 palette): body brass #ba6156 (lit faces #f2a65e), hopper copper #eb564b (shade #b0305c), coin gold #ffe478, gear and chute darker brass #8c3f5d, key steel #7e7e8f (lit #c2c2d1).
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
