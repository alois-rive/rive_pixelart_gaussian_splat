A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a small, chubby, friendly dragon sitting upright on its haunches, wings half raised, as if perched on top of a tower.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- Each side view is exactly frontal to its side: no other face of the object shows in it.
- The four panels show the SAME object, only turned: FRONT: the dragon faces the camera. RIGHT: the camera has walked round to the dragon's own right-hand side, so the dragon faces the LEFT edge of the image. BACK: seen from behind. TOP: seen from straight above, looking down, with the dragon's face towards the BOTTOM edge of the image. Each detail appears only in the views from which it is really visible.
- One flat, uniform, medium grey background (#9a9a9a) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views):
- A round, pear-shaped body sitting upright: 1.1 m wide, 1.0 m deep, 1.2 m tall, with a lighter belly plate on its front.
- A big chunky head on a short thick neck, on top of the body: head 0.8 m wide, 0.9 m long front to back, 0.6 m tall, with a short blunt snout pointing FORWARD, two small stubby horns pointing up and back, two large round eyes. The top of the head is 2.0 m above the ground.
- Two bat-like wings rising from the shoulders, half raised and spread to the sides and slightly back: each wing 0.9 m long, the whole wingspan 2.6 m in the FRONT view, the wing tips at 2.1 m height. Three blunt finger points on each wing's outer edge, chunky, no thin membranes lines.
- Two short thick hind legs folded under the body at the front, with big feet; two small arms held against the belly.
- A thick tail coming out at the back, curling round to the dragon's RIGHT side on the ground and ending in a small spade tip beside its right foot.
- A row of three small blunt spikes along the back of the neck.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 500 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no tiny details, no texture noise, no grain, no scales drawn on it.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours: body, head, legs, tail #96fbc7 (mint green); belly plate and wing membranes #ffb3cb (pink); horns, spikes and claws #f7ffae (pale yellow); eyes and nostrils #74569b (purple); the shaded faces may use #74569b.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
