A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a chubby cartoon pond frog sitting, ready to hop.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- Each side view is exactly frontal to its side: no other face of the object shows in it (a slightly oblique view comes out wider and fails the aspect gate).
- The four panels show the SAME frog, only turned: FRONT: the frog faces the camera. RIGHT: the camera has walked round to the frog's own right-hand side (not the image's right), so the frog faces the LEFT edge of the image. BACK: seen from behind. TOP: seen from straight above, looking down, with its head towards the BOTTOM edge of the image. Each detail appears only in the views from which it is really visible.
- One flat, uniform background (#9a9a9a, a mid grey) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views):
- A squat sitting frog 0.5 m wide, 0.6 m long (head to rump), 0.4 m tall to the top of its eyes.
- The body: a wide, blocky, faceted wedge, low at the front (the wide flat head with a broad mouth line) and rising to a rounded rump at the back.
- Two big bulging eyes on top of the head, at the front corners, each a faceted ball 0.14 m across, sticking up above the head line.
- Two front legs: short straight blocky arms under the chin, with flat three-toed feet on the ground.
- Two big back legs folded at the sides of the body (thigh blocks pointing forward, folded shins), with long flat webbed feet on the ground beside the body, reaching forward to the middle of the body.
- A pale belly and throat seen in the FRONT view under the mouth.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 300 to 500 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no tiny details, no texture noise, no grain, no spots.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours (the Sweetie 16 palette): back, head and legs lime green #a7f070 (lit) and #38b764 (shade), belly and throat pale yellow #ffcd75, eyes pale yellow #ffcd75 with a small dark #1a1c2c pupil, mouth line #257179, feet #38b764.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
