A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a circus tightrope walker standing upright, holding a long balance pole level across his body in both hands.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- Each side view is exactly frontal to its side: no other face of the object shows in it.
- The four panels show the SAME object, only turned: FRONT: the walker faces the camera. RIGHT: the camera has walked round to the walker's own right-hand side, so the walker faces the LEFT edge of the image and the pole is seen end-on as a small square in front of his waist. BACK: seen from behind. TOP: seen from straight above, looking down, with the walker's face towards the BOTTOM edge of the image and the pole running left to right. Each detail appears only in the views from which it is really visible.
- One flat, uniform, saturated blue background (#3d6be0) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels. The whole object must fit inside each panel with a margin: the pole must not touch the panel edges.

The object (identical in all four views):
- A slim figure 1.7 m tall standing with feet together (the feet one behind the other is not needed): legs 0.85 m, torso 0.6 m, head 0.25 m.
- A small round cap with a short brim on the head. A long scarf tied at the neck whose two ends hang down his back to the waist.
- Arms bent at the elbows, both hands in front of the waist at 1.0 m height, holding the pole.
- The balance pole: a straight thick rod 2.6 m long, level, centred on the walker, crossing in front of the waist; a small round weight on each end.
- Soft slippers.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render, like a chunky PlayStation-era game character: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, simple block shapes, about 400 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no tiny details, no folds, no texture noise, no grain.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours: shirt and trousers #63787d (teal grey) with a #8ea091 (sage) sash at the waist; cap, scarf and slippers #8b4049 (red brown); face and hands #caa05a (ochre); pole #c9cca1 (cream) with end weights #ae6a47 (rust); hair #543344 (dark plum); shaded faces may use #515262 or #543344.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
