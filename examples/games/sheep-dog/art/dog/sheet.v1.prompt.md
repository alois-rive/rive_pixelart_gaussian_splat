A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a chunky, toy-like cartoon sheepdog (a ginger and white collie) standing on all four legs.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- Each side view is exactly frontal to its side: no other face of the object shows in it (a slightly oblique view comes out wider and fails the aspect gate).
- The four panels show the SAME object, only turned: FRONT: the object faces the camera. RIGHT: the camera has walked round to the object's own right-hand side (not the image's right), so the object faces the LEFT edge of the image. BACK: seen from behind. TOP: seen from straight above, looking down, with its front towards the BOTTOM edge of the image and no wall showing. Each detail appears only in the views from which it is really visible.
- One flat, uniform, mid grey background (#bdbdbd) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views):
- A blocky body 0.30 m wide, 0.62 m long (front to back) and 0.26 m tall, its underside 0.26 m above the ground.
- Four short straight block legs 0.09 m square, 0.30 m tall, one at each corner of the body.
- A big blocky head 0.26 m wide, 0.28 m deep, 0.24 m tall at the front of the body, raised so its top is 0.78 m above the ground, with a short box muzzle 0.14 m wide sticking 0.12 m forward, a dark nose block at its tip.
- Two triangular ears, pointing up and slightly outward, 0.10 m tall, on top of the head at its left and right edges.
- A thick bushy tail, a tapered block 0.32 m long, sticking backwards and up at 40 degrees from the back of the body.
- The ONLY asymmetric detail: a cream patch over the dog's LEFT eye region (on the image's right side in the FRONT view).

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 300 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no fur strands, no tiny details, no texture noise, no grain.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours (DawnBringer 16): body, head top, ears, legs and tail fur ginger orange #d27d2c (shade #854c30); chest bib, muzzle, paws, tail tip and the eye patch cream white #deeed6; nose and eyes dark #140c1c.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
