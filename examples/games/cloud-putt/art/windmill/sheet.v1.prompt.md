A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a whimsical mini-golf windmill obstacle with a putting tunnel through its base.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- Every view sits whole inside its panel with a clear margin of background all round (at least a tenth of the panel): nothing touches or crosses a panel edge. The whole windmill with its sails spans at most three quarters of a panel's height.
- Each side view is exactly frontal to its side: no other face of the object shows in it (no roof top or ground surface visible in the FRONT, RIGHT and BACK views: they are flat elevations).
- The four panels show the SAME object, only turned: FRONT: the face with the sails faces the camera; the four sails form a big diagonal X (each sail at 45 degrees) in front of the tower, and the dark tunnel arch at the bottom centre shows between the two lower sails. RIGHT: the camera has walked round to the side that was on the RIGHT of the image in the front view, so the sails are seen edge-on as one thin vertical bar standing just in front of the tower on the LEFT side of the image, with the hub axle sticking out to the left; the tower is seen from the side with no tunnel arch on this side. It is NOT a copy of the front view. BACK: seen from behind: the tower's back with the dark tunnel exit arch at the bottom centre, the sails' X partly visible behind the tower's edges (mirrored). TOP: seen from straight above, looking down: the eight-sided cone roof as an octagon with its point in the middle, the sails seen edge-on as a thin horizontal bar along the BOTTOM side of the octagon (the front is towards the BOTTOM edge of the image).
- One flat, uniform, mid grey background (#9a9a9a) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views), sizes in metres:
- Tower: an eight-sided tapering tower 2.2 m across at the ground and 1.6 m across at its top, 2.4 m tall, coral pink walls.
- A white trim band 0.2 m tall around the top of the tower walls.
- Roof: an eight-sided cone 2.0 m across at its base and 1.2 m tall, blue, with a small yellow ball 0.25 m across on its point. Total height 3.85 m.
- Tunnel: a rounded arch opening 0.8 m wide and 0.75 m tall at the bottom centre of the FRONT face, running straight through to the same arch on the BACK face; dark inside.
- A small round window 0.35 m across in each of the left and right side walls, 1.3 m up.
- Hub: an orange square block 0.35 m wide on the front face at 2.0 m height, with a short axle.
- Four sails on the hub, in a diagonal X: each sail is a dark red wooden spar 0.1 m thick and 1.85 m long from the hub centre, carrying a flat white cloth panel 0.5 m wide and 1.3 m long on its outer part. The sails stand 0.25 m in front of the front wall. Their tips come down to about 0.7 m above the ground at 45 degrees.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 500 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no bricks, no wood grain lines, no tiny details, no texture noise, no grain.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours: tower walls coral pink #ff6c7a (lit) and #dc435b (shade); trim band and sail cloth white #fafffc with shade #a3c0e6; roof blue #448de7 with shade #3f48c2; finial ball yellow #ffeb33; hub orange #f58931; sail spars dark red #a63d57; tunnel and window insides dark #36354d.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
