# The Manor (the human hall), phase 1: blockout. One primitive per named part at the spec's sizes
# (metres, measured off sheet v1 at 184 px a metre). Up Z, facing -Y (the door), origin on the
# ground under the plinth's centre. The tower stands at the object's left-front corner (+X, -Y).
new
add mesh cube --name Plinth --size 1 --location 0 0 0.135
scale 2.4 2.0 0.27
