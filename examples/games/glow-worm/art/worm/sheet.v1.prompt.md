A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a chubby glowing cave worm (a glow-worm larva), the player character of a cave game: a head followed by four round body beads in a straight line.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and resting on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- Every view sits whole inside its panel with a clear margin of background all round (at least a tenth of the panel): nothing touches or crosses a panel edge.
- Each side view is exactly frontal to its side: no other face of the object shows in it (a slightly oblique view comes out wider and fails the aspect gate).
- The four panels show the SAME object, only turned: FRONT: the worm faces the camera head-on, so we see only its face (eyes, mouth, feelers) with the beads hidden straight behind the head. RIGHT: the camera has walked round to the worm's own right-hand side, so the worm's head points to the LEFT edge of the image and the beads trail off to the right, all five parts in one straight horizontal row. BACK: seen from behind: the smallest tail bead towards the camera, the bigger beads and the head peeking round it. TOP: seen from straight above, looking down, the worm lying straight with its head towards the BOTTOM edge of the image and the tail towards the top. Each detail appears only in the views from which it is really visible.
- One flat, uniform, mid grey background (#9a9a9a) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no glow halo, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

- No legs, no tail spike, no mouth teeth.

Style, keep exactly:
