A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game character: a small round old fisherman in a yellow raincoat and a yellow sou'wester hat, sitting on an upturned bucket.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- Each side view is exactly frontal to its side: no other face of the object shows in it (a slightly oblique view comes out wider and fails the aspect gate).
- The four panels show the SAME object, only turned: FRONT: the object faces the camera. RIGHT: the camera has walked round to the object's own right-hand side (not the image's right), so the object faces the LEFT edge of the image. BACK: seen from behind. TOP: seen from straight above, looking down, with its front towards the BOTTOM edge of the image and no wall showing. Each detail appears only in the views from which it is really visible (a part seen side-on in one view is seen end-on in another).
- One flat, uniform, mid grey background (#9a9a9a) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views):
- An upturned teal bucket 0.5 m across and 0.45 m tall, standing on the ground, a little wider at its bottom than its top.
- The fisherman sits on it, facing FRONT: a round chunky body in a yellow raincoat, 0.7 m wide and 0.6 m tall, sitting on the bucket.
- His legs bend forward over the front edge of the bucket, thighs level, shins straight down, ending in big dark pink rubber boots standing on the ground in front of the bucket.
- His two arms reach FORWARD and meet in front of his chest at about 0.85 m height, the hands (pink, chunky) together, as if gripping a fishing rod (no rod drawn).
- A round pink head 0.35 m across with a big bushy cream-white beard covering its lower half and a round nose.
- A yellow sou'wester rain hat: a rounded crown on the head and a wide brim 0.6 m across that slopes down and is longer at the BACK, over his neck.
- Total height 1.3 m.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 500 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no tiny details, no texture noise, no grain, no fishing rod, no fish.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours (an 8-colour palette, Pollen8): raincoat and hat #ffe07e (lit) and #ee6a7c (shade), face and hands #ffa7a5, beard #ffe7d6, boots #ab5675, bucket #34acba with lighter faces #72dcbb, eyes #73464c.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
