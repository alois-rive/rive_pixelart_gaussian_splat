A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game character: a small, stout greenhouse keeper with a huge round straw sun hat, a chunky toy-like figure.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the character centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- Every view sits whole inside its panel with a clear margin of background all round (at least a tenth of the panel): nothing touches or crosses a panel edge.
- Each side view is exactly frontal to its side: no other face of the character shows in it.
- The four panels show the SAME character, only turned: FRONT: the character faces the camera. RIGHT: the camera has walked round to the character's own left-hand side (the side on the RIGHT of the image in the front view), so the character faces the LEFT edge of the image; its nose and the watering-can spout point left. BACK: seen from behind. TOP: seen from straight above, looking down, with the character's front towards the BOTTOM edge of the image; from above the round hat brim covers almost everything.
- One flat, uniform, mid grey background (#9a9a9a) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The character (identical in all four views), 0.85 m tall in all:

- A tiny mint watering can held in the character's LEFT hand only (on the RIGHT side of the image in the front view), its spout pointing forward.

Style, keep exactly:
