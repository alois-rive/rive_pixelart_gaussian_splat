A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a sneaky cartoon alley cat standing on all four legs, the player character of a night rooftop game.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- Every view sits whole inside its panel with a clear margin of background all round (at least a tenth of the panel): nothing touches or crosses a panel edge, including the tail and the ears.
- Each side view is exactly frontal to its side: no other face of the object shows in it (a slightly oblique view comes out wider and fails the aspect gate).
- The four panels show the SAME cat, only turned: FRONT: the cat faces the camera (we see its face, both eyes, both ears, its two front legs in front of the two back legs, the tail rising behind the body). RIGHT: the camera has walked round to the cat's own right-hand side, so the cat's head points to the LEFT edge of the image and its tail is at the RIGHT. BACK: seen from behind (the tail towards the camera, rising straight up, the ears seen from behind). TOP: seen from straight above, looking down, with its head towards the BOTTOM edge of the image and the tail towards the top. Each detail appears only in the views from which it is really visible.
- One flat, uniform, light grey background (#d9d9d9) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views), about 1.0 m long from nose to rump (tail not counted), 0.36 m wide, 0.62 m tall to the ear tips:
- Body: a chunky faceted box-like torso 0.62 m long, 0.32 m wide, 0.26 m tall, its top 0.44 m above the ground, slightly higher at the shoulders than at the hips.
- Head: a faceted block 0.30 m wide, 0.26 m long, 0.24 m tall at the front of the body, a little above the shoulders; a short wedge muzzle at its front.
- Two big pointed triangular ears on top of the head, 0.12 m tall, wide apart.
- Two big almond eyes on the head's front, glowing mint green.
- Four straight thick legs, 0.08 m square, 0.20 m long, one under each corner of the body, with small block paws.
- A thick tail (0.09 m square) rising from the rump straight up and curling forward at its tip like a question mark, its top 0.75 m above the ground.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 500 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no fur strands, no whiskers, no tiny details, no texture noise, no grain.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours (a 6-colour palette, Cyclope6): fur dark wine red #631b34 (lit) and deep plum #411d31 (shade); inner ears, the muzzle and the paws slate #32535f; eyes mint #30e1b9.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
