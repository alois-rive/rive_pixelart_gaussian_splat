Game parts (`p2_form.txt` joins them, `p3_<part>.txt` exports each alone at its pivot): `Body` (origin
on the ground), `ArmL` / `ArmR` (shoulder pivots at x +-0.72, z 1.78), `LegL` / `LegR` (hip pivots at
x +-0.29, z 0.62). The game places the limbs at the body's transformed pivots (`api.transform`).
Cylinder caps buried in a neighbour, or facing down below the 1.6 m eye, are deleted in `p2_form.txt`
(the `non-manifold-edges` of `inspect stats` are those open caps): about 40 % fewer golem splats.

Assumptions: front leads. The views disagree on the rim's depth: the top view draws it 1.23 m across,
the side view 0.99 m (and the urn 1.10 m); the rim is 1.15 m across its flats as a compromise. The
side view's fist and feet sit a little forward of the body, as the top view shows. The rune, the visor
and the band stand 6 to 9 cm off the clay (the sheet paints them flush): closer than a splat spacing
(0.06 m) the clay's splats swallow them (the first scene renders showed a cyan smear).

Budgets: 520 triangles; 4 113 splats on the bench at the scene's spacings (clay 0.06, band and moss
0.05, glow 0.035). In the scene each of the 5 pooled golems costs about 3 900 splats with its crack
overlay.
