A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a small two-stage lunar lander spacecraft on four splayed landing legs.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- Each side view is exactly frontal to its side: no other face of the object shows in it (a slightly oblique view comes out wider and fails the aspect gate).
- The four panels show the SAME object, only turned: FRONT: the object faces the camera. RIGHT: the camera has walked round to the object's own right-hand side (not the image's right), so the object faces the LEFT edge of the image. BACK: seen from behind. TOP: seen from straight above, looking down, with its front towards the BOTTOM edge of the image and no wall showing. Each detail appears only in the views from which it is really visible (a part seen side-on in one view is seen end-on in another).
- One flat, uniform, mid grey background (#9a9a9a) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views):
- The descent stage: a squat octagonal box 1.3 m across and 0.55 m tall, its bottom 0.55 m above the ground, wrapped in tan foil.
- Four landing legs, one at each diagonal corner of the descent stage, splayed outwards and down, each a thick strut ending in a round flat foot pad 0.3 m across standing on the ground; the four pads sit on a square 2.2 m wide.
- A thruster nozzle: a dark bell-shaped cone under the centre of the descent stage, 0.45 m across at its open bottom, its bottom 0.15 m above the ground.
- The cabin (ascent stage) on top: a chunky faceted box 1.0 m wide, 0.85 m deep and 0.7 m tall, pale cream, with a big dark triangular window on its FRONT face and a small square hatch below the window.
- A dish antenna on a short mast on the cabin's top, on the lander's own RIGHT side (the image's left in the FRONT view): a shallow dish 0.4 m across facing up and outwards.
- Total height 2.0 m.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 500 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no tiny details, no texture noise, no grain, no writing or flags.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours (an 8-colour palette, Nyx8): descent stage foil #997577 (lit) and #816271 (shade), cabin #c3a38a with lighter faces #f6d6bd, legs, foot pads and nozzle #4e495f, window #0f2a3f, dish #f6d6bd.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
