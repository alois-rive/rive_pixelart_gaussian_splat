A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a small, chunky, retro cartoon rocket ship standing upright on its four fins.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- Every view sits whole inside its panel with a clear margin of background all round (at least a tenth of the panel): nothing touches or crosses a panel edge.
- Each side view is exactly frontal to its side: no other face of the object shows in it (a slightly oblique view comes out wider and fails the aspect gate).
- The four panels show the SAME object, only turned: FRONT: the rocket faces the camera, its round window centred on the body. RIGHT: the camera has walked round to the side that was on the RIGHT of the image in the front view, so the window is now on the LEFT edge of the body, seen edge-on as a thin bump. BACK: seen from behind, no window. TOP: seen from straight above, looking down on the nose tip, with the window side towards the BOTTOM edge of the image; the four fins form a plus sign. Each detail appears only in the views from which it is really visible.
- One flat, uniform, mid grey background (#9a9a9a) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views):

Style, keep exactly:

- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours: fuselage #ffffff (lit) and #c5cddb (shade); nose cone, band and fins #da2424 (lit) and #720d0d (shade); window glass #5ee9e9 with a rim #8b97b6; nozzle #353658.
