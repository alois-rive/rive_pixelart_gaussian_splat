A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a paper sky lantern (a floating festival lantern), unlit.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- Each side view is exactly frontal to its side: no other face of the object shows in it (a slightly oblique view comes out wider and fails the aspect gate).
- The four panels show the SAME object, only turned: FRONT: the object faces the camera. RIGHT: the camera has walked round to the object's own right-hand side (not the image's right), so the object faces the LEFT edge of the image. BACK: seen from behind. TOP: seen from straight above, looking down, with its front towards the BOTTOM edge of the image and no wall showing. Each detail appears only in the views from which it is really visible.
- One flat, uniform, mid grey background (#9a9a9a) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views):
- A square paper body, 0.50 m wide at the top, tapering to 0.38 m wide at its open bottom, 0.55 m tall, its bottom edge 0.10 m above the ground line.
- On top, a low four-sided paper pyramid roof 0.50 m wide and 0.14 m tall.
- Four dark thin vertical ribs (0.04 m wide) running down the four corners of the body.
- A dark square frame ring 0.05 m tall around the open bottom edge.
- Two dark thin wires crossing the open bottom in an X, and hanging from their crossing a small square fuel block 0.10 m wide and 0.10 m tall whose bottom touches the ground line.
- A wide dark paper band 0.06 m tall around the body, 0.12 m below the top edge.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 150 triangles worth of detail. No smooth surfaces, no wrinkles, no tiny details, no texture noise, no grain, no glow, no light rays.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours (Midnight Ablaze): paper deep rose #7c183c (shade #460e2b), ribs, band, bottom frame and wires very dark #31051e, the fuel block coral #ff8274.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
