A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a chubby cartoon garden mole popping half out of the ground, front paws raised.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- Each side view is exactly frontal to its side: no other face of the object shows in it (a slightly oblique view comes out wider and fails the aspect gate).
- The four panels show the SAME mole, only turned: FRONT: the mole faces the camera. RIGHT: the camera has walked round to the mole's own right-hand side (not the image's right), so the mole faces the LEFT edge of the image. BACK: seen from behind. TOP: seen from straight above, looking down, with its nose towards the BOTTOM edge of the image.
- One flat, uniform background (#d9d9d9, a pale grey) everywhere: no floor, no ground, no hole, no dirt, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views):
- The upper half of a round, plump mole standing upright, cut flat at the bottom where it leaves the ground: 0.5 m wide, 0.45 m deep, 0.6 m tall.
- The body: a faceted, slightly pear-shaped upright blob, widest at the bottom, rounding into the head at the top (no neck).
- A big pointed snout sticking forward from the front of the head, 0.15 m long, with a large round nose ball 0.1 m across at its tip.
- Two tiny dark eyes just above the snout.
- Two big flat spade-shaped front paws with three thick claws each, held up at the sides of the snout, palms facing forward, 0.18 m across.
- A small pale belly patch on the front.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 300 to 500 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no fur strands, no tiny details, no texture noise, no grain.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours (the Steam Lords palette): fur dusky purple #433a60 (lit) and #2f213b (shade), snout and paws #775c4f, nose ball #a19f7c, claws #c0d1cc, belly #4f5277, eyes #170e19.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
