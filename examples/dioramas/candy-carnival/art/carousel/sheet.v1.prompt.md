A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a small toy fairground carousel (merry-go-round).

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- FRONT: the object faces the camera. RIGHT: the camera stands at the object's right side, so the object faces the LEFT edge of the image. BACK: seen from behind. TOP: seen from straight above with its front towards the BOTTOM edge of the image.
- One flat, uniform, pale grey background (#d9d9d9) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views):
- A round platform 3.0 m across and 0.3 m tall (an 8-sided disc), standing on the ground.
- A thick central column 0.5 m across rising from the platform centre to the canopy.
- Six thin vertical poles in a ring 1.1 m from the centre, from the platform to the canopy; on each pole a chunky blocky horse about 0.8 m long and 0.6 m tall, all horses facing the same way round the ring (counter-clockwise seen from above). Three horses sit higher (0.9 m above the platform), three lower (0.5 m), alternating.
- A wide, shallow cone canopy 3.2 m across: a vertical skirt band 0.35 m tall at its rim, at 2.4 m above the ground, then a cone rising to 3.2 m, with eight broad alternating stripes running from the rim to the top.
- A small ball and a little pennant flag on top of the cone, total height about 3.6 m.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 400 to 700 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no tiny details, no texture noise, no grain.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours (the 16-colour PICO-8 palette): canopy stripes red #ff004d and cream #fff1e8, canopy skirt band yellow #ffec27, platform side blue #29adff with a top of orange #ffa300, central column pink #ff77a8, poles yellow #ffec27, horses cream #fff1e8 with pink #ff77a8 saddles and dark #7e2553 manes, flag green #00e436, top ball yellow #ffec27.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
