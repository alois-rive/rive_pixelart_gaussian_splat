A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a small crooked stone crypt chapel in a haunted graveyard.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- FRONT: the object faces the camera. RIGHT: the camera stands at the object's right side, so the object faces the LEFT edge of the image. BACK: seen from behind. TOP: seen from straight above with its front towards the BOTTOM edge of the image.
- One flat, uniform, pale grey background (#d9d9d9) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views):
- A narrow stone chapel body 1.8 m wide, 2.6 m deep and 1.8 m tall to the eaves, on a low plinth 0.2 m tall and a little wider.
- A steep gabled roof, ridge running front to back, 1.3 m tall above the eaves, overhanging the walls a little.
- On the FRONT gable a small square bell tower 0.6 m wide that rises 1.0 m above the ridge with its own little pointed roof, the whole tower leaning noticeably to the RIGHT side of the chapel (crooked).
- A tall pointed-arch door 0.6 m wide and 1.2 m tall in the middle of the front wall, and one narrow pointed window glowing pale in the middle of each long side wall.
- Two chunky buttresses on each long side wall.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 300 to 600 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no tiny details, no texture noise, no grain.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these four colours (the Hollow palette): stone walls, tower and buttresses mid grey-blue #565a75, roofs and plinth near-black #0f0f1b, door near-black #0f0f1b with a frame of pale #c6b7be, glowing windows white #fafbf6, trim along the eaves and gable edges pale #c6b7be.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
