A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a small radar dish antenna on a pedestal, for a sci-fi research outpost.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- FRONT: the object faces the camera. RIGHT: the camera stands at the object's right side, so the object faces the LEFT edge of the image. BACK: seen from behind. TOP: seen from straight above with its front towards the BOTTOM edge of the image.
- One flat, uniform, pale grey background (#d9d9d9) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views):
- A squat octagonal pedestal 1.0 m wide and 0.6 m tall standing on the ground.
- On top of it a turntable drum 0.7 m wide and 0.25 m tall, and a U-shaped yoke: two upright arms 0.15 m thick, 0.9 m apart, rising 0.7 m above the drum.
- Between the yoke arms, held by a horizontal axle at about 1.35 m height, a round, shallow, faceted dish (12 sides) 2.0 m across and 0.35 m deep. The dish's open concave face points FORWARD (towards the FRONT view) and is tilted UP by about 30 degrees, so in the FRONT view it reads as a slightly squashed round disc, and in the RIGHT view it reads as a thick tilted bowl seen edge-on.
- A feed horn: three thin struts from the dish rim meeting at a small box receiver 0.7 m in front of the dish centre, with a small glowing tip.
- A boxy counterweight 0.5 x 0.3 x 0.3 m on the back of the dish.
- The whole object is about 2.3 m tall and symmetric left to right.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 300 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no tiny details, no texture noise, no grain, no bolts, no panel lines.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours (an 8-colour green monochrome palette, Ammo-8): dish inside #89a257, dish back and counterweight #4d8061, pedestal and yoke #305d42 with edges #4d8061, feed struts and receiver #1e3a29, glowing tip #eeffcc.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
