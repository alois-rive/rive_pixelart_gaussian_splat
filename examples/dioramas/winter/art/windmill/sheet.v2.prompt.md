A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a small wooden post windmill in winter.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- FRONT: the object faces the camera. RIGHT: the camera stands at the object's right side, so the object faces the LEFT edge of the image. BACK: seen from behind. TOP: seen from straight above with its front towards the BOTTOM edge of the image.
- One flat, uniform, pale grey background (#d9d9d9) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views):
- A square wooden mill house about 1.6 m wide, 1.6 m deep and 1.8 m tall, standing on a short stone base 0.4 m tall.
- A steep gabled roof covered in a thick cap of snow, ridge running front to back, total height about 3.2 m.
- Four broad lattice sails on a hub at the FRONT of the house, about 1.4 m above the base; the sails form an X (diagonals) and reach about 1.5 m from the hub.
- A small door on the front under the sails, a little square window on each side.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes. No smooth organic surfaces, no tiny details, no texture noise, no grain.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours (a 16-colour palette, Magik16): wood planks #591e37 #9b5a44, stone base #3c4348 #668b90, snow #bee5e1 #fbfceb, sails #e9cea8 with spars #591e37, door #1c1e1f, glowing windows #f0f3c4.
- About 300 to 600 triangles worth of detail.
