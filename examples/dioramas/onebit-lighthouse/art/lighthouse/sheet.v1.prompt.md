A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a small coastal lighthouse for a 1-bit (two-colour) game.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- FRONT: the object faces the camera. RIGHT: the camera stands at the object's right side, so the object faces the LEFT edge of the image. BACK: seen from behind. TOP: seen from straight above with its front towards the BOTTOM edge of the image.
- One flat, uniform, mid grey background (#9a9a9a) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views):
- A tapered 8-sided tower, 1.6 m across at the bottom and 1.1 m across at the top, 4.0 m tall, standing on a low 8-sided stone plinth 2.0 m across and 0.4 m tall.
- Two broad dark bands painted around the white tower, each 0.5 m tall, at 1.4 m and 2.8 m above the ground.
- On top of the tower a gallery: a flat 8-sided deck 1.6 m across, 0.15 m thick, with a simple solid dark parapet 0.25 m tall around its edge.
- On the gallery the lantern room: an 8-sided glass room 0.8 m across and 0.7 m tall, the glass white, with a dark 8-sided pointed roof 0.5 m tall and a small ball on top. Total height about 6.0 m.
- On the FRONT of the tower: a small dark door 0.5 m wide and 0.9 m tall at the bottom. On the RIGHT side only: one small dark window 0.25 m square, 2.2 m up. The back and left are plain.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 300 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no tiny details, no texture noise, no grain, no outlines.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these two colours (the 1bit Monitor Glow palette): white parts #f0f6f0 (tower, plinth, lantern glass), dark parts #222323 (bands, parapet, roof, ball, door, window).
- Even light, so each face reads as one flat tone; no shading gradients, no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
