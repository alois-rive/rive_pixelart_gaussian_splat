A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a small old stone well.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- FRONT: the object faces the camera. RIGHT: the camera stands at the object's right side, so the object faces the LEFT edge of the image. BACK: seen from behind. TOP: seen from straight above with its front towards the BOTTOM edge of the image.
- One flat, uniform, pale grey background (#d9d9d9) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views):
- A round well ring of chunky grey-blue stone blocks, about 1.2 m wide and 0.7 m tall, 8 sided, with a few blocks of moss on its rim.
- Two square wooden posts rising from opposite sides of the ring (left and right in the front view), joined at the top by a horizontal crossbeam with a crank handle on the right end.
- A small gabled roof on the posts, two flat red-tiled planes, ridge running left-right in the front view, overhanging the ring slightly. Total height about 2.2 m.
- A wooden bucket hanging from a rope under the crossbeam, inside the ring.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 300 to 800 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no tiny details, no texture noise, no grain.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours (a 16-colour palette, Fading 16): stone #645355 #8c7c79 #a99c8d, wood posts #645355 #846d59, beams and bucket #846d59 #a88a5e, roof tiles #774251 #9c524e, moss #5b7d73 #8e9f7d, rope #cca87b, dark gaps #4b3d44.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
