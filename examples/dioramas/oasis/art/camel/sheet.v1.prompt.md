A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game character: a chunky, friendly desert camel, standing in a neutral pose (all four legs straight, head forward).

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- One flat, uniform, pale grey background (#d9d9d9) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.
- In the FRONT view the camel faces the camera; in the RIGHT view it faces to the right of the image.

The character (identical in all four views):
- Body about 1.8 m long, 0.7 m wide; one big hump on the back; total height about 2.0 m at the head.
- A long curved neck rising forward, a blocky head with a short snout, small ears.
- Four straight legs, each two simple segments (upper and lower) with flat feet, a short tail.
- A woven saddle blanket over the hump, with a simple stripe.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes. No smooth organic surfaces, no tiny details, no texture noise, no grain.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
- Built from simple boxy segments a 3D artist can rig: separate readable leg segments, neck, head.
- Flat colour areas only, from these colours: fur #cd683d #e6904e #fbb954, darker hooves and snout #9e4539 #694f62, blanket #4d65b4 #4d9be6 #f9c22b, eyes #2e222f.
- About 400 to 800 triangles worth of detail.
