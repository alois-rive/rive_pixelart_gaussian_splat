A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a small farm barn for a 4-colour Game Boy style game.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- FRONT: the object faces the camera. RIGHT: the camera stands at the object's right side, so the object faces the LEFT edge of the image. BACK: seen from behind. TOP: seen from straight above with its front towards the BOTTOM edge of the image.
- One flat, uniform, pale grey background (#d9d9d9) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views):
- A box-shaped barn body 3.0 m wide (left to right in the FRONT view), 3.6 m deep (front to back) and 2.0 m tall to the eaves.
- A simple gabled roof with straight slopes, ridge running FRONT to BACK, ridge 3.2 m above the ground; the roof overhangs the walls by 0.2 m on every side, so in the FRONT view the gable is a triangle on top of the wall.
- On the FRONT wall: a big double barn door 1.4 m wide and 1.6 m tall, centred, with a light X brace on each leaf; above it, in the gable triangle, a small square hay-loft door 0.6 m.
- On the BACK wall: one small square window 0.5 m, centred, 1.3 m up. No door at the back.
- On the RIGHT side wall only: one small square window 0.5 m, in the middle, 1.1 m up. The LEFT side wall is plain.
- Light corner boards on the four vertical wall corners.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 150 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no tiny details, no texture noise, no grain, no plank lines.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these four colours (the Kirokaze Gameboy palette): barn walls #46878f, roof #332c50, door X braces, corner boards, loft door frame and window frames #e2f3e4, door leaves and window glass #332c50.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
