A modeling reference sheet (turnaround) for ONE low-poly 3D game asset: a giant clam, half open, with a glowing pearl inside.

Canvas and layout, keep exactly:
- 1536 x 1024 landscape image, a 2 x 2 grid of four equal panels.
- Top-left panel: FRONT view. Top-right: RIGHT side view. Bottom-left: BACK view. Bottom-right: TOP view (looking straight down).
- Orthographic cameras (no perspective), the same scale in every panel, the object centred in each panel and standing on the same invisible ground line in the three side views.
- FRONT: the object faces the camera. RIGHT: the camera stands at the object's right side, so the object faces the LEFT edge of the image. BACK: seen from behind. TOP: seen from straight above with its front towards the BOTTOM edge of the image.
- One flat, uniform, pale grey background (#d9d9d9) everywhere: no floor, no cast shadow, no gradient, no vignette, no frame lines, no text, no labels.

The object (identical in all four views):
- A giant clam 2.0 m wide (left to right) and 1.5 m deep (front to back), its hinge at the BACK.
- The lower shell: a shallow bowl 0.5 m tall whose front rim is a big zig-zag of five wide scallop teeth.
- The upper shell: the same shape, hinged at the back, lifted open about 40 degrees, so its zig-zag front rim stands 1.3 m above the ground in the FRONT view. Both shells have five broad radial ribs fanning out from the hinge.
- Inside, between the shells, a round pearl 0.35 m across sitting on a soft inner lip (the mantle), visible from the front.

Style, keep exactly:
- A clean low-poly 3D model render: faceted, flat-shaded faces, hard straight edges, big readable chunky shapes, about 300 triangles worth of detail. No smooth organic surfaces, no tiny details, no texture noise, no grain.
- Flat colour areas only, ONLY these colours (a 4-colour palette, BLK AQU4): shell #00b9be (lit) and #005f8c (shade, the grooves between ribs), the inner mantle #002b59, the pearl #9ff4e5.
- Soft, even light from the upper left, so each face reads as one flat tone; no specular highlights, no ambient occlusion blur.
